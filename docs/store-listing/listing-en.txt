TITLE (max 30):
ArtDaily

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80):
A different masterpiece every day, on your home screen. No ads.

FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000):
ArtDaily shows you a different artwork every day — public-domain paintings
and prints from The Metropolitan Museum of Art, the Art Institute of
Chicago, the Cleveland Museum of Art, and the Rijksmuseum.

No ads. No account or sign-in. No tracking. Works offline once the catalog
is downloaded.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Discover a new artwork every day, picked from thousands of paintings and
  prints across four open-access museum collections.
• Home screen widget — add several at once, each with its own filters.
• Filter by period, art movement, artist, or year range.
• Save your favorite artworks and revisit them anytime.
• Set any artwork as your wallpaper, manually or with automatic rotation
  through your favorites.
• Read each artwork's curatorial description, with on-device translation
  into other languages (nothing is ever sent to a server).
• See full artwork details: artist, year, medium, source museum, and
  license.

ABOUT THE ARTWORKS

Every artwork is public domain or published by its museum under an open
license (CC0 or CC BY), with attribution shown on its detail page.

PRIVACY

ArtDaily does not collect personal data. Your favorites, history, and
preferences stay on your phone only. Full privacy policy:
https://pacohurtadof.github.io/art-daily/
